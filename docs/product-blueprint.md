# Product blueprint

The product promise is: “Discover the Vim techniques experienced developers rely on—and practice them until they become part of how you edit.”

The audience understands basic Vim but is not yet fluent with precise, composable edits. The experience should be approachable, encouraging, discovery-oriented, and never punitive about hints or alternate valid solutions. Correctness comes first; coaching follows. Equivalent or unknown-but-correct approaches pass.

The eventual loop introduces a useful technique, provides realistic practice, varies its application, removes clues over time, interleaves it with other skills, and resurfaces it at decreasing frequency. Mastery means automatic selection and transfer, not mere recognition. C++, CMake, and shell are the initial content domains.

The MVP is static and local-first: no backend, accounts, runtime AI, paid API, hosted database, mandatory streak, leaderboard, or social system. Versioned local persistence retains compact evidence and session position; export/import remains deferred. Two narrow units cover precise text objects, then line-local targeting and operator composition. Prerequisites guide rather than lock access.

Domain logic must remain framework-independent. Exercise outcomes will be declarative and validated. Correct final state is authoritative when strategy recognition is uncertain. Every future reference solution must replay from the initial state and satisfy its outcome rule.

The engine gate promises fidelity only for taught behavior. If clean reset, observation, or central taught behavior requires extensive patches, reconsider the engine before product construction. Do not claim complete Vim/Neovim compatibility.
